A low angle must look low; a Dutch angle must tilt; a crash zoom is drawn at the end of the zoom with motion blur radiating from the subject; an orbital move is drawn mid-arc with the background streaked; a whip pan is drawn as a horizontal smear frame with the destination subject sharp.
- `motion` describes the anime motion language of the panel: smears, impact frames (a single high-contrast frame at the moment of contact), speed lines radiating from the point of impact, hair/cloth follow-through, debris, dust, light flares. Draw these as drawing - NEVER as text.
- `sakuga_tier` 3 panels are the showpiece cuts of the sheet: push the foreshortening, the perspective and the motion drawing further than the rest.
