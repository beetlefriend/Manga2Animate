01

What it does概要

Manga2Animate turns a few manga pages and a character reference into an animation that stays faithful to the source: an animation script, then a visual storyboard, each written by a creator agent and checked against the panels by a reviewer agent over two rounds, and only then a video. Faithful is the point, and also the limit: the output can only ever reuse the manga's own panel compositions.

Sakuga Kit keeps that pipeline as ground truth and adds a fourth layer on top of it. A Shot Expansion Agent re-stages the verified script with new camera angles and inserts the cuts the manga never drew: low-angle impacts, crash zooms, whip pans, reaction close-ups, impact frames. A Continuity Reviewer checks every new shot against the source panels and keeps, revises or drops it. The result is a longer, more cinematic shot list that still contains nothing the manga does not support, drawn into a creative storyboard and animated with per-shot camera direction.

The kit is open source. The compute it burns is paid for by the Production Fund: trading fees on $SAKUGA buy generation credits, the credits make cuts, and the ledger is public.

4agents added

Shot Expansion Agent, Continuity Reviewer, Creative Storyboard Reviewer, Character Sheet Reviewer, next to the paper's creator and reviewer pairs.

6 → 12shots per chapter

Six faithful panels become six, nine or twelve shots depending on the creativity dial. Every faithful beat is kept; inserts are silent.

02

How it works工程

Three faithful stages from the paper, then the creative layer, then video. The reviewers are what keep the creative layer honest: every agent that invents has a paired agent that checks the invention against the panels.

03

Shot vocabularyカメラ

What the Shot Expansion Agent is allowed to propose. Each one is grounded in a source panel: the camera moves, the world does not. Hover a card to see the move.

04

Creativity dial演出度

One setting decides how far the kit may move from the page. Six panels always stay; the dial only decides how many shots are added around them.

  • Public-domain manga and comics (Hokusai's manga volumes qualify).
  • Original work, submitted by its author with a written licence.
  • Work under a permissive licence (CC BY, CC BY-SA) with attribution.

09

Questions質問

Is this the original authors' project?

No. Sakuga Kit is an independent kit built on the open-source Manga2Animate code by Sheng-Wei Zhang, Hsi-An Chen, Yan-Ting Liou, Chih-Hsuan Chen, Shih-Chen Tseng and Yu-Lun Liu (National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University). Their paper, page and code are linked above; the faithful pipeline is theirs, the creative layer and the studio are ours. They are not involved in the token.

What does Creative Expansion change?

Only the camera. The faithful script, verified against the manga by the paper's reviewers, stays the ground truth. The Shot Expansion Agent may re-angle a clip or insert a silent in-between moment; it may not add characters, change wardrobe, add events, reorder beats or write dialogue. The Continuity Reviewer checks every shot against the panels and the faithful script and returns keep, revise or drop.

Which models does it use?

Gemini for every text and image agent (creators on Flash, reviewers on Pro). Seedance 2.x on BytePlus Ark or through Higgsfield for the paper's reference-conditioned route; Seedance i2v or Kling 4.0 for one clip per shot; MiniMax H3 for 2K. The open models from the paper (FLUX.2-dev, MiniMax-H3, AniSora) run too, on big GPUs. The repo's docs/MODELS.md has the lineup.

What does the token actually do?

It funds compute. A fee on every trade goes to the Production Fund; the Fund buys the credits the kit burns; the ledger shows fees in, credits bought and what each cut cost. Holders vote on the queue. It does not entitle anyone to revenue or to the cuts, which are released publicly under their source's licence. Not financial advice.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Clone the repo, put a Gemini key in .env, drop a few pages and a reference image under target/<case>/, and run python src/full_pipeline.py -t <case> --creative --dry-run first. The dry run prints every prompt and every video request without spending anything.
